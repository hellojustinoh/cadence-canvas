Three drills.
Ninety seconds a day.

Each drill isolates one muscle of spoken thought. Your mic does the work — Cadence does the measuring.

01

Sprint

60 seconds · pace

Get a prompt, talk for a minute, keep the words flowing. Trains you to generate speech at the speed of thought without stalling.

02

Filler Hunt

45 seconds · cleanliness

A live counter ticks every time you say like, you know, basically, I mean… Watch it. Hate it. Starve it.

03

Distill

30 seconds · economy

One idea, one breath. Land the point before the clock does. Trains the TL;DR reflex that voice-first AI rewards.
