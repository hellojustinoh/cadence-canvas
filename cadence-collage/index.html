Why out loud?

Because voice is becoming the keyboard, and nobody taught us to type with our mouths.

The math

You speak 5× faster than you type

Around 220 words a minute speaking versus 45 typing. Every voice interface, from Claude to Siri to Wispr Flow, is a bet on that gap. Cadence trains you to actually cash it in.

220wpm talking
45wpm typing
The catch

Speed is worthless if it arrives as a ramble

The keyboard let you edit before anyone saw your words. Voice has no backspace. The edit has to happen in your head, and that is a trainable skill, not a personality trait.

"So basically what I was trying to say is, like, the thing we talked about, you know the one…"nobody, ever again, hopefully
