# Cadence tenets (inferred)

No tenets document existed in the repo, in Notion, or in the branch history, so
these were derived from the README's stated premise and the public collage
app's copy on 2026-10-01. Treat them as a draft to confirm, not as settled.

1. **The drill is the product.** Every screen exists to get you into, through,
   or back from a scored drill. Anything that doesn't earn its tap on that path
   goes.
2. **Sixty seconds, one honest number.** Drills are short enough to do daily.
   The score is legible, consistent run to run, and explained in one sentence.
3. **Awareness is the cure.** The app shows you the filler as it leaves your
   mouth. It coaches; it never scolds.
4. **Nothing leaves the browser.** No account, no upload, no server. History
   lives on the device and survives upgrades.
5. **Same engine, any skin.** Scoring, drills, and storage are shared across the
   six treatments. Engine changes should be portable; copy changes are per-skin.
6. **Fast from fresh.** Fresh install to first scored drill is measured in taps
   and seconds, and both numbers only go down.

Decisions that change scoring, onboarding, or notifications ship behind the
test-build switch (`?build=test`) until the owner turns them on.
