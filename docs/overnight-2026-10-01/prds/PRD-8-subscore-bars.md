# PRD-8 · Sub-score bars on results

**Tenet:** 2. **Switch:** none (visual only). Drawn and critiqued.

## Problem
The results screen shows a score and four raw stats, but the score is a
weighted blend of three sub-scores (pace, cleanliness, flow) the user never
sees. "An honest little scoreboard" should show its working.

## Proposal
Three short bars under the score ring: Pace 50%, Clean 30%, Flow 20% (weights
per mode) with the sub-score value. One line each.

## Measure
No effect on taps or seconds.

## Risks
More to read on the one screen that should be glanced at. The reviewer may cut
this; I'm not confident it earns its space.
