TELEMETRY

Every rep is measured in-browser by your machine's speech engine. Three signals, one composite score.

SIG/01PACE
0WPM TARGET BAND 130–190

Below 110 reads as hesitant. Above 210, articulation degrades. The band is where authority lives.

SIG/02FILLERS
0PER 100 WORDS, TARGET <2

Each filler is a packet of nothing. Replace with silence: pauses read as thought, fillers read as doubt.

SIG/03STALLS
0GAPS >2 SEC, TARGET 0

Dead air means the sentence was launched without a landing site. Decide the ending first.

04

PROTOCOL

  1. P/1

    CALIBRATE

    Run one Sprint cold. That score is your baseline. Do not warm up; the ugly number is the useful one.

  2. P/2

    DOSE DAILY

    One drill per day, rotating. Ninety seconds. Consistency beats duration by an embarrassing margin.

  3. P/3

    READ THE TAPE

    Your transcript flags every filler. Read it once out loud, clean. That re-read is half the training effect.

  4. P/4

    DEPLOY

    Dictate your next message to an AI instead of typing it. Field conditions are the final drill.
